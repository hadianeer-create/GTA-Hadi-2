# HADI: VICE CITY — Story Bible

Original story, characters and setting. Tone: sun-bleached crime drama with heart. Think warm family
moments, neon nights, fast cars, and a city being sold out from under the people who live in it.

## Logline

Three years after a getaway job went wrong and his father died the same night, **Hadi** comes home to Vice
City. A luxury developer is buying Coral Key block by block, his family's marina is the last holdout, and
the people who framed him are still running the show.

## Characters

* **Hadi Karam (29).** Lebanese-Cuban, born and raised on Coral Key. Ex-getaway driver, gifted mechanic, quietly
  funny, fiercely loyal. He left after "the Port fire" and has never forgiven himself.
* **Samira Karam.** Hadi's mother. Runs the **Coral Cafe** on Seabreeze Boulevard and feeds half the island on credit.
* **Elias Karam (deceased).** Hadi's father, owner of **Karam Marine** at Pier 3. Officially drowned the night of the
  fire.
* **Rafael "Rafa" Ortega.** Hadi's best friend, a charter-boat captain at Pier 3. Big laugh, bigger debts.
* **Luz Marín.** Investigative reporter at the *Vice City Ledger*, chasing Vance Horizon's land deals.
* **Celeste Vance.** CEO of **Vance Horizon**, the developer rebuilding Coral Key as "Horizon Key". Charming,
  patient, ruthless.
* **Nico Varela.** Owner of the club *Velvet Static* on Neon Mile. Celeste's muscle, and the man who ran the Port job.
* **Detective Imani Rhodes.** VCPD. Reopened the Port fire on her own time. Doesn't trust Hadi, and doesn't
  trust her captain either.
* **"Gator" Boone.** Sawgrass Flats fixer and smuggler who pays well and asks for loyalty.
* **Dez Alvarado.** Barrio Palmera street-race organiser and Hadi's former rival (side missions).

## Structure

### Prologue: Fresh Start ✅ playable
1. **Welcome Home.** Hadi wakes at the Seagrape Apartments and has breakfast at the Coral Cafe. Samira gives him
   his father's keys.
2. **Old Friend.** The Vento GT his father kept running for him is parked outside.
3. **Pier 3.** Rafa: *"Look who's back. Get some rest, hermano. Tomorrow we talk business."*

### Act I: Salt & Neon
4. **Charter Season.** Run a sunset charter with Rafa (boat driving tutorial).
5. **Interest Rates.** Nico's men smash Rafa's boat. Hadi learns Rafa owes Velvet Static $20,000.
6. **Velvet Static.** Infiltrate Nico's club during a launch party (stealth and social).
7. **Eviction Notice.** Vance Horizon posts demolition notices on Marina Row. Hadi meets Luz, who is photographing them.
8. **Heat Run.** A street race in Barrio Palmera for prize money (racing tutorial; Dez).
   * **Choice 1:** pay Rafa's debt (lose money, Nico stays friendly) **or** steal the ledger that proves it (keep the
     money, gain heat and Nico's hatred).

### Act II: High Tide
9. **The Ledger** / **The Payment** (branch).
10. **Sawgrass.** Gator Boone offers smuggling work. An airboat chase through the flats.
11. **Paper Trail.** With Luz, break into Vance Horizon's site office at night.
12. **Port of Vice.** Return to the warehouse where it happened. A flashback cutscene shows the job was a setup.
13. **Rhodes.** The detective arrests Hadi, then lets him go with a deal: bring her proof.
14. **Ridge Run.** Chase Nico's courier along Coquina Ridge's cliff roads.
15. **What the Sea Took.** Divers find Elias's boat. It was scuttled, not sunk in a storm.
    * **Choice 2:** give the evidence to **Luz** (public exposure) **or** sell it to **Gator** (money and a powerful ally).

### Act III: Hurricane Season
16. **Category Four.** A hurricane hits Vice City (dynamic weather showcase). Rescue Samira and the cafe
    regulars by boat and by car.
17. **Horizon.** Celeste's launch gala at Vance Horizon HQ during the storm's eye. Confront Nico on the roof.
18. **Last Holdout.**
    * **Choice 3 / endings:**
      * **Daylight** (Luz path): Celeste is indicted and Karam Marine survives. Hadi testifies about the Port job
        and does six months.
      * **Kingmaker** (Gator path): Hadi takes Nico's place and buys the waterfront. Rich, feared and alone.
      * **Open Water** (any path): Hadi, Samira and Rafa sell up and sail south. Coral Key changes forever, but
        they're free.
    * **Epilogue:** a post-storm sunrise drive along Seabreeze Boulevard that reflects your choices.

## Side content
* **Neon Flamingos:** 12 hidden in Coral Key ✅. Later districts add their own collectibles.
* **Charters & taxi fares:** passenger missions with tips for smooth driving.
* **Street races:** Dez's circuit across all districts.
* **Lifeguard rescues:** swimming and boat rescues on Coral Bay Beach.
* **Properties:** Karam Marine (boat garage), a Neon Mile club (income), a Coquina Ridge villa (safehouse),
  a Barrio Palmera garage (vehicle customization).
* **Strangers:** short character stories, e.g. a retired deco architect fighting the demolitions, or a radio DJ
  looking for a stolen master tape.

## Cutscenes (Sequencer, Phase 8)
Opening flight over Vice City · Samira's kitchen · Velvet Static reveal · the Port fire flashback ·
Elias's boat raised from the bay · the hurricane landfall · three ending cinematics.
